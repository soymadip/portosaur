Show several images in one slot — a screenshot gallery, before and
after shots, a feature showcase. One slide shows at a time, and the
reader moves between them with arrows, dots, the keyboard, or a swipe.

{/* prettier-ignore */}
```jsx
<Carousel>
  ![First screen](/img/carousel-1.svg)
  ![Second screen](/img/carousel-2.svg)
  ![Third screen](/img/carousel-3.svg)
</Carousel>
```

The example above, live:

## Raw images

Markdown images and raw `<img>` tags work the same as slides, and
either way the alt text shows as a caption under the image. Raw
images do not open the zoom viewer — only Markdown images do.

```jsx
<Carousel>
  <img src="/img/carousel-1.svg" alt="First screen, as a raw tag" />
  <img src="/img/carousel-2.svg" alt="Second screen, as a raw tag" />
</Carousel>
```

The example above, live:

## Cards as slides

Anything can be a slide, not just images. A whole `<Cards>` grid
makes one slide, or a single `<Card>` on its own — though one card
stretches to the full slide width, so a grid per slide usually looks
better.

```jsx
<Carousel>
  <Cards>
    <Card
      title="Install"
      description="Get the CLI on your machine."
      icon="lu:download"
    />
    <Card
      title="Scaffold"
      description="Start a new site in seconds."
      icon="lu:folder-plus"
    />
  </Cards>
  <Cards>
    <Card title="Write" description="Author pages in MDX." icon="lu:pen-line" />
    <Card title="Build" description="Ship the static site." icon="lu:hammer" />
  </Cards>
</Carousel>
```

## Behavior

- **Captions.** A slide that is a bare image shows its alt text as a
  caption underneath. A `<figure>` slide keeps its own caption and
  gets no automatic one.
- **One image per slide.** Consecutive Markdown images become
  separate slides even without blank lines between them — each
  image is its own slide.
- **Autoplay.** `autoplay={5}` moves to the next slide every five
  seconds. It pauses while the pointer is over the carousel or
  anything inside it has focus, and resumes after. `0` turns it off.
- **Looping.** The arrows wrap from the last slide back to the first.
  With `loop={false}` they stop at the ends instead.
- **Keyboard.** Left and right arrows move between slides when the
  carousel has focus. Home and End jump to the first and last slide.
- **Swipe.** Drag left or right on touch to switch slides.
- **One slide.** A carousel with a single slide shows no arrows or
  dots — there is nothing to switch between.
- **Reduced motion.** With reduced motion on, slides switch instantly
  instead of sliding.

## Props Reference

### `<Carousel />`

| Prop         | Type        | Default | Description                                   |
| ------------ | ----------- | ------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| `showArrows` | `boolean`   | `true`  | Show the prev/next arrow buttons.             |
| `showDots`   | `boolean`   | `true`  | Show the dot indicators under the slides.     |
| `autoplay`   | `number`    | `0`     | Seconds between slides. 0 turns autoplay off. |
| `loop`       | `boolean`   | `true`  | Wrap from the last slide back to the first.   |
| `children`   | `ReactNode` | —       | The slides, one element each.                 |
